### Voice notes (so future copy stays consistent)
- **Spare, not chatty.** Short lines. Let white space carry weight.
- **Confident, never hype.** No "revolutionary," no "cutting-edge." Say the true thing plainly.
- **Noble-modern duality.** Words like *considered, deliberate, crafted, worth, built to last* — paired with *modern, fast, ships.*
- **Independent, not small.** A studio that works anywhere, to a premium standard.
